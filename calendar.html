Fifteen teaching weeks, two lecture sessions per week. Problem sets are released on the Friday of the week shown and are due one week later.

Lecture Schedule

Table C.1. Weekly topics, readings and deliverables.
WeekTopicsReadingDue
1 Scope of materials handling; the ten principles; classification of handling equipment; unit load concept Ch. 1.1–1.3—
2 Belt conveyor components; belt construction; bulk material characterisation; capacity calculation Ch. 1.4–1.7PS 1
3 Belt speed selection; motion resistances; drive power by ISO 5048 / DIN 22101 Ch. 1.8–1.9PS 2
4 Belt tension distribution; Euler–Eytelwein drive limit; take-up systems; belt sag; belt strength selection; conveyor geometry Ch. 1.10–1.16PS 3
5 Chain and apron conveyors: chain pull, sprocket selection, drag and en-masse conveyors Ch. 2—
6 Screw conveyors: flight geometry, fill factor, capacity, power, hanger bearings Ch. 3PS 4
7 Bucket elevators: centrifugal vs. continuous discharge, bucket spacing, head geometry Ch. 4—
8 Midterm examination (Chapters 1–4); project teams and duties assigned —Midterm
9 Pneumatic conveying I: dilute phase, saltation velocity, system layouts Ch. 5Project proposal
10 Pneumatic conveying II: pressure drop, blower selection, separation and dust control Ch. 5PS 5
11 Cranes and hoists I: duty classification, wire rope, sheaves and drums Ch. 6—
12 Cranes and hoists II: hoist and travel drives, girder loading, stability, safety devices Ch. 6PS 6
13 Industrial trucks: stability triangle, load charts, fleet sizing; AGV guidance and traffic control Ch. 7—
14 Storage systems: rack types, AS/RS cycle time, order picking, warehouse layout Ch. 8—
15 Life-cycle costing and equipment justification; project presentations; course review Ch. 8Project report
