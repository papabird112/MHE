Note

The course opens with equipment selection so that every later chapter can be read as an answer to a selection question already asked. Chapter 3 then spans three weeks because it establishes the calculation framework — capacity, resistance, power, tension — that the remaining chapters reuse in modified form. Time invested there pays off for the rest of the course.
